# Piste

A timeline of everyone who has been to space, and of the Stations they lived aboard.

## Language

### People and flights

**Person**:
Someone who has been past the US 50-mile / 80 km line: astronaut, cosmonaut, taikonaut, payload specialist or paying customer alike.

**Flight**:
One continuous stretch of a Person's time in space, launch to landing, even when it chains several Missions (a Soyuz up, a seat-swap down).
_Avoid_: trip

**Mission**:
The name of one launch or craft a Flight is made of, e.g. "Soyuz MS-24". A Flight with a seat-swap has two.

### Stations

**Station**:
An orbiting habitat people have lived aboard: Salyut 1, 3, 4, 5, 6 and 7, Skylab, Mir, the ISS, Tiangong-1, Tiangong-2 and Tiangong. Stations that never had a crew (Salyut 2, Kosmos-557, Genesis) aren't Stations here. The current Chinese station is plain "Tiangong".
_Avoid_: space station (in names), CSS, Chinese Space Station, Tiangong space station

**Stay**:
A Person's time aboard one Station, from the docking of the craft they arrived in to the undocking of the craft they left in. Moving a craft to another port doesn't end it, and docking without going aboard (Soyuz 10 at Salyut 1) isn't one. One Flight usually makes one Stay; Soyuz T-15 made three (Mir, Salyut 7, Mir).
_Avoid_: visit, residency, increment

**Expedition**:
A Station's long-duration resident crew for one continuous stretch, under whatever name that Station gave it: Expedition 72 (ISS), EO-27 (Mir), Skylab 4, Shenzhou-19 (Tiangong). Its dates are crew handovers, not any one Person's Stay. A Visitor crew is never an Expedition, even when a source calls it one.
_Avoid_: increment, principal expedition, EO, visiting expedition, EP

**Expedition crew**:
The Stays that belong to an Expedition. A Stay can belong to two (Pesquet's covered Expeditions 50 and 51), and whether it belongs is decided by membership, not by the craft it arrived in.
_Avoid_: resident crew, long-duration crew

**Visitor**:
A Person on a Stay that belongs to no Expedition: Shuttle crews docked at Mir or the ISS, week-long Soyuz crews, Interkosmos cosmonauts, private-mission crews and tourists. It describes the Stay, not the Person: Scott Kelly was a Visitor on STS-118 and Expedition crew later.
_Avoid_: guest, taxi crew, visiting expedition, spaceflight participant
